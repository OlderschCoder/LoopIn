LoopIn

Private safety technology for real life.

Technology already tracks where people go, what they click, who they talk to, and what they do next. LoopIn turns that idea around. It uses modern tools to protect the person, not monetize them.

LoopIn is your private safety layer for dating, nights out, rides, meetups, campus life, travel, work shifts, public transportation, parking lots, and the moments when something feels off but is not yet an emergency.

It is not a dating app add-on.

It is the app that stays with you after the introduction.

What LoopIn Does

LoopIn helps you protect your identity, notice red flags, capture important details, check in with people you trust, and get help when something changes.

Virtual BFF

LoopIn acts like the friend who helps you think clearly before, during, and after a meetup. It can help review profiles, messages, plans, screenshots, and behavior patterns so you can spot pressure, inconsistency, manipulation, scams, boundary testing, or other red flags before they become bigger problems.

Private Line

Use a real phone number for calls and chat without giving out your personal number too early. Keep early communication safer, more controlled, and separate from your everyday identity.

Safety Vault

Save the details that matter: who you are meeting, where you are going, screenshots, notes, profile information, phone numbers, plans, timelines, and changes as they happen. Your information stays private by default.

Check-Ins

Set check-in times before a date, ride, shift, walk, trip, or meetup. Confirm you are okay, update your status, or let LoopIn escalate if you miss a check-in.

Walk With Me

Start a temporary safety session when walking to your car, leaving work, crossing campus, taking transit, riding home, or getting out of an uncomfortable situation. Your trusted people do not need to track you all the time. They need to know when something changes.

Code Phrase

Use a normal-looking phrase that quietly signals LoopIn to take action. This gives you a safer option when you cannot openly say you need help.

Trusted Crew

Choose the people who should be looped in if something goes wrong. You control who is included, what they can see, and when they are notified.

Emergency Loop

If you tap for help, miss a check-in, or use a code phrase, LoopIn gives your trusted crew the information they need to act: where you are, who you are with, what changed, recent activity, and how to reach you.

Evidence Timeline

LoopIn helps preserve context as events unfold, including notes, check-ins, communication history, safety actions, and key details. You do not have to reconstruct everything later from memory.

Why It Matters

Dating apps help people connect. Social apps help people communicate. Maps help people move. Phones help people call.

But none of them were built to protect the whole person across the messy middle of real life.

LoopIn brings those pieces together into one private, user-controlled safety layer.

Technology should work for you, not just on you.

LoopIn uses it for you.
