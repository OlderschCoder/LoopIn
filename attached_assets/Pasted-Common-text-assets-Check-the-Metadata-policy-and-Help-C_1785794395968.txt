Full description

Safety that stays with you. After the introduction.

LoopIn is private safety technology for real life — dates, rides, campus walks, travel, and meeting anyone new. Nine tools. One safety layer.

🔒 Masked Private Line Text and call anyone without ever sharing your real number. Get a dedicated private number for dating apps, marketplace meetups, or anyone you're not ready to trust with your real contact info.

⏰ Check-Ins & Safety Timer Heading out? Set a check-in. If you don't confirm you're safe, your Trusted Crew is automatically alerted with your last known location and your plans.

🚶 Walk With Me Walking home or taking a ride? Start a timer. If you don't confirm arrival, alerts go out automatically.

👥 Trusted Crew Build your circle of trusted contacts. They get automatic alerts, your location, and your status — only when it matters.

🆘 One-Tap SOS Emergency button that calls 911, alerts your Trusted Crew, and guides you through calming breathing exercises — all in one tap.

📞 Fake Call Escape Need an exit? Schedule a realistic incoming call to gracefully leave any uncomfortable situation.

🤖 AI Safety Coach Your 24/7 wingwoman. Get help spotting red flags in dating profiles, planning safe first meetings, and talking through anything that feels off.

🗂️ Safety Vault Securely save voice notes, call recordings, and screenshots in a private, searchable timeline. Your evidence, organized and protected.
